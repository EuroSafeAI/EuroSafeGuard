# Security policy

EuroSafeGuard is a safety system, so a reliable bypass is a security issue. If you find a prompt, suffix or technique
that gets harmful requests or replies past the guardrail, do not publish it, and do not open a public issue.
Report it privately through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" button (Security tab of this repository). Include the
model and threshold files you used (seed, mode, your own thresholds if any), the category of the harm, and how often
the bypass works. Do not include more harmful content than needed to reproduce it.

We will confirm receipt within a week, and we credit reporters in the release notes unless they ask us not to.
Over-refusals (benign requests that are blocked) are not security issues: open a normal issue for those, without
sensitive personal data.
